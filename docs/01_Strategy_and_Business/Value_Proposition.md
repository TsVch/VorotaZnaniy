## 🎯 For Content Creators (Authors, Experts, Companies)

### Pains
- Piracy: A purchased PDF is posted on torrents or Telegram channels 5 minutes after purchase.
- Lack of analytics: The author doesn't know if the buyer read the book, what chapter they dropped off at, what was useful.
- Integration complexity: Existing DRM solutions require complex setup or ruin UX, reducing returns and reputation.

### Gains
- **Smart protection**: Dynamic watermark with buyer ID and session, limit on concurrent devices. Pirated copy becomes "toxic" (easily traceable), while legitimate copy is convenient.
- **Deep analytics**: Dashboard with data on which chapters are read most often, where users close the document, how much time they spend.
- **Seamless integration**: "Buy" button or widget that embeds into existing sites in 5 minutes via simple script or integration with Webflow/WordPress.

### Unique Selling Proposition (USP) for authors:
> "Turn your static PDF into an interactive, protected, and analyzable digital asset that can't be stolen unnoticed."

## 🎯 For Content Buyers (Students, Professionals)

### Pains
- Purchased file is inconvenient to read on phone.
- Hard to find needed information in a large guide or book.
- Fear of losing access to purchased materials (if author deletes file from Google Drive).

### Gains
- **Unified library**: All purchased materials in one place, with progress sync between devices.
- **AI assistant**: Ability to ask a question about the text ("How do I set up this template?") and get an accurate answer with a link to the document page.
- **Interactive learning**: Automatically generated flashcards and quizzes to test knowledge based on what was read.
- **Certificates**: Automatic generation of completion certificate (for educational content).

### Unique Selling Proposition (USP) for buyers:
> "Don't just download a file. Get a personal AI tutor who knows the material you purchased inside and out, and access to it forever."

## 🛡️ Why Piracy Becomes Unprofitable (Economic Protection)
We can't make copying impossible (screenshots are always possible), but we make the legitimate copy **so much more valuable** than the pirated one that purchasing becomes the rational choice:
1. Pirated copy doesn't have access to AI assistant.
2. Pirated copy doesn't get updates (author can update the document, and legitimate user gets new version, while pirate doesn't).
3. Pirated copy doesn't provide completion certificate.
4. Dynamic watermark on every screen de-anonymizes the leak source, deterring mass sharing.
