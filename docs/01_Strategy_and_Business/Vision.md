## 🌟 Mission
Give digital content creators full control over their intellectual property, turning the process of protecting and distributing knowledge from a technical headache into a competitive advantage, while enriching the end consumer experience with personalized AI.

## 🔭 Vision Statement
Become the de facto standard for distributing premium digital knowledge on the internet. A platform where authors are confident their revenue is protected from piracy, and buyers receive not just a "file," but an interactive, intelligent, and secure environment for learning and work.

## 🧭 Strategic Principles
1. **Protection without UX compromise**: We don't use aggressive DRM that breaks the experience for legitimate users (e.g., blocking all text copying or preventing offline reading where it's critical). We use smart methods: dynamic watermarking, session/device binding, concurrent session limits.
2. **AI as added value, not just a feature**: AI assistant, quizzes, and summaries are generated *strictly* in the context of the purchased document. This increases the value of a legitimate copy compared to a pirated one (where AI context is absent).
3. **Scalability from day one**: The architecture is designed so that scaling from 100 users to 1,000,000 doesn't require rewriting the core system.
4. **Ecosystem independence**: The platform should easily integrate with authors' existing tools (Notion, Gumroad, Stripe, Telegram), not force them to completely change their workflow.

## 🎯 Long-term Goals (3-5 years)
- Support 100,000+ active content creators.
- Ensure 99.99% uptime for the document viewing service.
- Reduce successful content piracy on the platform by 80% compared to standard file distribution via cloud drives.
- Enter the Enterprise segment (corporate training, internal company documentation).

## 🚫 What We DON'T Do (Out of Scope)
- We don't create a social network or content marketplace (at the initial stage). We provide a SaaS tool (B2B2C) that authors embed in their sites/blogs.
- We don't engage in legal prosecution of pirates. Our task is to technically and economically (through UX) complicate piracy.
- We don't store or train our AI models on user data without their explicit consent. Author data privacy is an absolute priority.
